## What Is the "Baggage Handler Burst Test"?

Standard hardshell suitcases rely on a nylon coil zipper to hold two plastic halves together. When squeezed, the flexible plastic shell deforms, putting extreme tension on the zipper seam. If the bag takes a hard impact, the zipper bursts open, spilling your contents across the tarmac.

An aluminum-frame carry-on solves this by replacing the zipper with a rigid aircraft-grade aluminum spine secured by heavy-duty lever latches. Instead of pulling apart under stress, the metal frame interlocks tighter under compression. It acts like a vault, protecting fragile glass dropper bottles, luxury cologne, and delicate makeup palettes from getting crushed—no matter how rough the handling gets.

## Common Luggage Mistakes to Avoid

1.  **Paying for Brand Prestige Over Hardware:** An $800 price tag often buys a logo, not better crash resistance. If a luxury suitcase uses a standard soft zipper, it is still vulnerable to bursting.
2.  **Overpacking Flexible Shells:** Stuffing a soft-sided or budget zippered bag flexes the shell outward, exposing glass skincare containers and delicate grooming devices to blunt impacts.
3.  **Ignoring Internal Compression:** A rigid outer shell only does half the job. Without sturdy internal divider straps, your face creams, electric shavers, and eye palettes will rattle around and shatter.
