## What to Look For Before You Buy

Before adding any appliance to your cart, consider these core features to ensure it matches Indian cooking habits:

*   **Capacity & Wattage:** For solo cooks or couples, a 3.5-liter to 4-liter capacity is plenty. For a family of four or more, look for 5.5 liters and above. Aim for at least 1400W to 1800W to ensure rapid heating for thick batters, marinated meats, and paneer.
*   **Heating System & Airflow:** Look for models featuring 360-degree rapid air circulation. Efficient airflow locks in moisture while crispening the exterior without drowning food in oil.
*   **App Integration & Smart Presets:** Modern appliances offer Wi-Fi connectivity and smartphone control. Pre-programmed Indian presets (for tikka, kebabs, pakodas, and reheating sweets) eliminate the trial and error.
*   **Non-Stick Quality:** Look for ceramic or PFOA-free, food-grade non-stick coatings. Just as you avoid harsh chemicals in your skincare regimen, you want clean, non-toxic surfaces touching your food at high heat.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1.  **Overcrowding the Basket:** Stacking food tightly traps steam, leaving you with soggy snacks instead of crisp ones. Cook in light, single layers.
2.  **Scratched Non-Stick Coatings:** Using metal tongs or abrasive scrubbers ruins internal basket coatings. Treat non-stick surfaces gently, much like exfoliating delicate skin on your face.
3.  **Ignoring Kitchen Ventilation:** Smart appliances need breathing room. Ensure you have 4 to 5 inches of clearance behind the air fryer exhaust to avoid overheating cabinet walls.
