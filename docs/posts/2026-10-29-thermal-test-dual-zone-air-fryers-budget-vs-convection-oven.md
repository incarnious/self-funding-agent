## 3 Common Air-Frying Mistakes That Ruin Your Finish

Even a high-performing unit can produce dull results if your prep routine is off. Avoid these three frequent blunders:

1.  **Overcrowding the Basket:** Just like layering three heavy facial creams at once stops your skin from absorbing any of them, packing a basket wall-to-wall stops thermal airflow. Give your food space to breathe.
2.  **Skipping the Light Oil Prep:** Cooking entirely oil-free is like putting liquid concealer on dry, un-prepped skin—it leaves a chalky, uneven surface. A light spray of avocado oil acts as a heat conductor for a golden, polished finish.
3.  **Ignoring the Mid-Cook Shake:** Thermal imaging proved that top-down heating creates a heat gradient. Shaking your baskets halfway through rotates the surface area, ensuring a uniform texture from top to bottom.
