## Key Features That Actually Matter

When shopping for a robot vacuum, don't get distracted by flashy app gimmicks. Focus on these core specs:

*   **LiDAR Navigation over Camera-Only:** LiDAR uses invisible lasers to map rooms in total darkness. It handles bathroom vanities, chair legs, and tight corners effortlessly without getting confused by morning shadows or glare off vanity mirrors.
*   **Sealed Suction Paths & HEPA Filtration:** If you frequently deal with cosmetics, face powders, or fine grooming dust, non-sealed dustbins will blow micro-particles right back into your air. Look for a fully sealed HEPA system.
*   **Self-Emptying Docks with High-Static Pressure:** Budget self-emptying docks have gotten incredibly good. A strong suction pull at the base ensures fine powder and dense hair clumps don't clog the internal bin port.
*   **Tangle-Free Rubber Rollers:** Traditional bristle brushes wrap long hair and beard trimmings around the axle. Single or dual rubber rollers shed hair into the suction channel far more reliably.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1.  **Assuming Higher Price Equals Better Suction:** Flagship models spend hundreds of dollars on dual-line lasers, active hot-water mop washing, and motorized drop-down arms. If your main goal is clean carpets and floor dust removal, a mid-range or budget vacuum often delivers higher raw suction for less money.
2.  **Skipping Filter Maintenance:** Micro-dust from makeup powders and dry shampoos clogs high-efficiency filters fast. Wash or replace your filters monthly to keep suction at peak levels.
3.  **Ignoring Threshold Heights:** High-tech obstacle avoidance can sometimes be *too* cautious, refusing to cross slightly raised bathroom thresholds or thick rug borders.
