## What Separates a Flex-Proof Shell from Disposable Plastic

When shopping in the accessible $100–$150 price bracket, material choice is the difference between a lifetime bag and disposable junk. 

*   **Polycarbonate (PC) vs. ABS:** Pure polycarbonate acts like a resilient skin barrier—it bends under severe impact and pops right back into shape. Cheap ABS plastic feels rigid in the showroom, but under pressure or in cold air, it shatters easily.
*   **Aesthetic & Scratch Resistance:** Choose textured or micro-diamond finishes over high-gloss shells. Much like applying a glowing highlighter over uneven skin texture, high-gloss plastic magnifies every single micro-scratch from airport security bins.
*   **Grooming-Safe Interior Layouts:** A smooth shell is useless if your inside gear gets crushed. Look for full-zip interior dividers rather than cheap elastic straps so your heavy dopp kit or delicate cosmetics tray stays locked in place.

## What to Check Before You Buy

1.  **Wheel Mounts:** Make sure the wheel housings are screwed into reinforced corner molds rather than glued directly to thin bottom plastic.
2.  **Zipper Gauge:** Look for heavy-duty #8 or #10 self-repairing zippers to prevent bursting under tension.
3.  **Wipeable Lining:** Spilled beard oil or leaked foundation shouldn't ruin your bag; look for water-resistant, easy-to-clean interior fabrics.
