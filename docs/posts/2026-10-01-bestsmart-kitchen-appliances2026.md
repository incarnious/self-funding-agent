## What to Look for in an Indian Smart Kitchen

Indian cooking presents unique challenges: heavy spice usage, high-heat sautéing, and diverse regional recipes. When evaluating smart appliances, look beyond basic Wi-Fi connectivity and focus on practical utility:

* **Localized Recipe Integration:** Look for companion apps that offer pre-programmed Indian cooking profiles (daals, biryanis, tikkas) rather than relying strictly on Western cook-times.
* **Physical Fallback Controls:** High-tech connectivity shouldn't leave you stranded during an internet outage. Ensure the appliance retains manual buttons or dials.
* **Power-Resume Memory:** In areas prone to voltage drops or power outages, your appliance should automatically resume its cooking cycle once power returns.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid When Upgrading

* **Buying "Smart" for Gimmicks Alone:** A smart kettle that only turns on via an app offers little value over a standard flip switch unless it includes precise temperature-holding for specialized teas or baby formula.
* **Ignoring Companion App Reviews:** An appliance is only as good as the software driving it. Check the app rating on Google Play or the Apple App Store before buying; buggy software creates frustration.
* **Neglecting Maintenance:** High-tech devices use sensitive sensors. Grease buildup from heavy Indian oil usage can block optical sensors in smart chimneys or air fryers, reducing performance over time.
