## What to Look For (And 3 Common Mistakes to Avoid)

Whether shopping for your first fryer or upgrading your current setup, focus on internal build quality over cosmetic gimmicks:

* **Basket Geometry:** Square baskets provide superior thermal reflection compared to round ones, offering more usable surface area for flat roasting.
* **Ceramic Coating:** Look for non-toxic ceramic non-stick linings. They withstand high thermal cycling better than traditional coatings, ensuring clean food release without degrading over time.

### Mistakes to Avoid:
1. **Overcrowding the Basket:** Smothering food starves the thermal vortex. Air fryers rely on convection; if air can't circulate around the food, you're simply baking it slowly.
2. **Ignoring Clearance:** Placing your fryer directly against a backsplash traps vented heat, which can crack quartz countertops or melt outlet covers. Always leave 5 inches of rear clearance.
3. **Overusing Oil:** Aerosol cooking sprays contain propellants that break down basket coatings. Use a simple oil mister filled with high-smoke-point avocado oil instead.
