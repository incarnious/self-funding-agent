## What to Look For: Key Features for Skin & Lungs

Before diving into performance, it helps to know what actually cleans the air versus what is just slick marketing.

*   **True HEPA (H13 or H14 Grade):** Avoid "HEPA-type" or "HEPA-like" claims. True H13 HEPA filters capture 99.97% of particles down to 0.3 microns. This is the sweet spot for capturing microscopic pet allergen proteins and smoke debris before they trigger skin inflammation or allergies.
*   **Granular Activated Carbon:** While HEPA catches solid dust and hair, activated carbon absorbs volatile organic compounds (VOCs), cooking fumes, and smoke odors. Look for thick, pelletized carbon beds rather than thin, carbon-coated pads.
*   **High Clean Air Delivery Rate (CADR):** Look for a CADR rating matched to your room size. A high fan volume ensures the air circulates through the filter multiple times an hour.

## Common Mistakes That Ruin Your Air Quality

1.  **Forgetting the Inner Plastic:** Millions run purifiers for months without realizing the inner filter is still sealed in factory plastic. Always unbox and unwrap your filter before powering on.
2.  **Neglecting the Pre-Filter:** Pet hair rapidly smothers the outer pre-filter mesh. Vacuum or rinse your pre-filter every two weeks to keep airflow strong and prevent motor strain.
3.  **Tucking Units into Corners:** Placing a purifier behind a couch or in a tight corner restricts 360-degree intake. Keep units at least 12 to 18 inches away from walls.
