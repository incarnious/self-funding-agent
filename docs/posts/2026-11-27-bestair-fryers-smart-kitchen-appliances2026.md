## Key Features to Look For Before Buying

1.  **PFOA/BPA-Free Non-Stick Baskets:** Always verify that internal coatings are free from toxic chemicals that can break down under high thermal stress.
2.  **Indian Preset Menus:** Look for smart appliances with dedicated settings for local staples like samosas, tikkas, kebabs, and roasted nuts.
3.  **Optimum Wattage (1400W–1800W):** Higher wattage ensures quick preheating and rapid air circulation, giving your food a golden crispy exterior without drying out the inside.
4.  **Dishwasher-Safe Removable Parts:** Maintenance should be painless. Detachable, non-stick baskets save time and prevent rancid oil build-up over time.

## 4 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

*   **Buying Too Small:** Don't buy a compact 2L unit to save a small amount upfront if you cook for a family; overcrowding the basket results in soggy, unevenly cooked food.
*   **Ignoring Service Networks:** Always choose brands with established service centers and accessible replacement parts across Indian cities.
*   **Focusing Only on Frying:** Don't limit your air fryer to snacks; use it to bake, reheat, and roast daily meal prep staples.
*   **Neglecting Countertop Space:** Measure your kitchen counter height and check socket placement before investing in multi-functional smart appliances.
