## What to Look For: Choosing "Active Ingredients" Over Packaging

When shopping for a digital lock, ignore shiny touchscreens and focus on the hardware specs that matter for daily protection and longevity.

* **Biometric Precision:** Look for **capacitive or semiconductor 3D sensors** with a False Acceptance Rate (FAR) below 0.001%. They work reliably even with dry, worn, or freshly moisturized hands.
* **Mechanical Grade Rating:** Ensure the core deadbolt carries an **ANSI/BHMA Grade 1 or Grade 2** certification. A smart lock is only as strong as its physical bolt.
* **Offline Resilience:** Look for locks featuring local encryption. If your internet goes down, your door should still open instantly via fingerprint, PIN, or a concealed mechanical key override.
* **Anti-Tesla Coil Protection:** Verify that the internal relays are shielded against strong electromagnetic interference.
