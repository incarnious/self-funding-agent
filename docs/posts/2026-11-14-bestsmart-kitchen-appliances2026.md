## What to Look For Before Upgrading Your Kitchen

Indian culinary habits demand heavy-duty performance. High heat, rich oil infusions, and precise multi-stage cooking mean off-the-shelf global tech doesn't always cut it. Keep these essentials in mind:

*   **Indian Recipe Calibration:** Look for appliances with preset smart programs tuned for regional dishes (like *dum* biryani, *tadka*, or slow-cooked *dals*).
*   **Robust App & Voice Integration:** Ensure the companion mobile app offers reliable Android and iOS support, along with seamless Hindi or English voice commands via Alexa or Google Assistant.
*   **Power Cut Auto-Resume:** In many Indian cities, brief power outages are common. Choose appliances with built-in memory functions so your cooking cycle resumes right where it stopped.
*   **Maintenance Simplicity:** Much like choosing light, non-comedogenic skincare products that won't clog your pores, pick appliances with detachable, dishwasher-safe components that won't trap grease and grime in hard-to-reach nooks.

## Common Mistakes First-Time Buyers Make

*   **Prioritizing Gimmicks Over Utility:** Avoid buying a smart toaster or kettle just because it has Bluetooth. Focus on appliances where remote monitoring genuinely saves time.
*   **Ignoring Service Network Availability:** High-tech components require trained technicians. Always verify that the brand has an active service center in your city.
*   **Overlooking Wi-Fi Dead Zones:** Kitchens tucked behind thick concrete walls often suffer from weak Wi-Fi signals. Check your kitchen's connectivity before installing app-dependent devices.
