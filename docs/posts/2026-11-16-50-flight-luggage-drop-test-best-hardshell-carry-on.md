## What Real Durability Looks Like (And What Most Travelers Miss)

When force-testing hardshell carry-ons, the point of failure is rarely the middle of the bag—it's the corners, the wheel housings, and the zippers under expansion stress. 

To understand luggage performance, think of shell materials like skincare formulas:
* **ABS Plastic (Cheap & Rigid):** Like a heavy powder foundation, ABS looks smooth initially but cracks easily under pressure. It offers zero give.
* **Aluminum (Luxury & Heavy):** Like a sleek metal vintage compact, it looks stunning out of the box, but a single drop leaves permanent dents and warps the frame alignment.
* **100% Polycarbonate (The Gold Standard):** Like a resilient, hydrating skin barrier, high-grade polycarbonate flexes upon impact and pops back into shape without cracking. 

If your bag contains fragile glass skincare bottles, hair tools, or delicate men’s grooming accessories, a flex-tolerant polycarbonate shell absorbs kinetic energy so your internal liquids don't take the hit.

## 3 Common Luggage Mistakes That Cost You at the Gate

1. **Buying ABS/PC Blends mistaking them for Pure Polycarbonate:** Manufacturers often label bags "Polycarbonate" when they are actually an ABS core with a thin PC film coating. Always check for 100% raw polycarbonate.
2. **Ignoring Wheel Base Recessing:** High-mounted spinner wheels snapping off is the #1 cause of dead luggage. Look for wheels built *into* the shell mold, not just screwed onto the exterior floor.
3. **Over-Packing Without Internal Compression:** Unsecured heavy dopp kits and shoe bags shift during turbulence, exerting outward pressure that bursts non-reinforced zipper teeth.
