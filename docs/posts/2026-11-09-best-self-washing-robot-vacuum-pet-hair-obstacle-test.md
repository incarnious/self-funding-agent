## Navigation Tech: Why Pure LiDAR Beats Overengineered AI

Expensive flagships often brag about dual RGB cameras and complex artificial intelligence. On paper, it sounds like a high-tech night cream with dozens of active ingredients. In practice, visual AI can be temperamental. RGB cameras struggle in low-light conditions under beds, get confused by dark rug patterns, and sometimes overthink simple obstacles. 

During our test, the $1,600 flagship’s camera mistook a dark shadow near the sofa for a drop-off, leaving an entire zone uncleaned, before eating a loose iPhone cable. 

In contrast, the $349 budget model relied on a top-mounted turret LiDAR system paired with simple physical bumper sensors. It mapped the room with millimetric laser precision, ignored lighting changes, and gently navigated around table legs without eating the cords. 

## What to Look For: Essential Features vs. Marketing Fluff

Building an efficient floor-care routine is a lot like streamlining a daily grooming regimen—focus on core ingredients that perform, not shiny packaging.

* **Dual Rubber Rollers over Bristles:** Bristle brushes absorb hair like a dry sponge absorbs liquid foundation. Always opt for solid rubber rollers. Hair glides off rubber and travels straight into the dustbin instead of wrapping around the core.
* **Automatic Mop Lifting (Minimum 10mm):** If your home has mixed flooring, a self-washing robot must lift its mop pads off the ground when traversing rugs. Look for at least 10mm of clearance so low-pile carpets stay completely dry.
* **Hot-Water Pad Washing:** Cold water rinses away surface dust, but hot water (130°F+) breaks down dried spills and pet oils. It keeps the mop pads fresh and prevents that sour towel smell.

## 3 Maintenance Mistakes Ruining Your Investment

Even top-tier hardware will fail if you treat it carelessly. Avoid these three common maintenance traps:

1. **Ignoring the Side Bearings:** Hair naturally wraps around the extreme ends of the main brush axle. If left unchecked, the friction generates heat that can melt the plastic roller housing. Pop the roller out weekly to clear these end caps.
2. **Using Standard Floor Cleaners:** Never add traditional tile or wood cleaner to the clean water tank. High-foaming soaps coat the internal water lines and blind the optical fluid sensors—similar to using body wash on your face, it leaves an unwanted film and causes irritation. Use only brand-approved, non-foaming solutions.
3. **Skipping Dock Tray Cleaning:** Even self-cleaning bases accumulate sludge beneath the wash plate. Rinse the tray basin every fortnight to prevent bacterial buildup.

## Which Tier Suits Your Home?

* **Budget Tier ($300–$450):** Perfect for apartments and hard floors. Look for laser LiDAR navigation, basic self-emptying, and solid rubber brushes. You skip hot-water washing, but you gain rock-solid navigation.
* **Mid-Range Tier ($500–$900):** The sweet spot for pet owners and multi-surface homes. This tier adds automatic mop lifting, auto-refilling water tanks, and heated pad drying.
* **Premium Tier ($1,200+):** Worth it only for massive, multi-story layouts requiring edge-extending mop arms, built-in voice assistants, or direct plumbing hookups for water lines.
