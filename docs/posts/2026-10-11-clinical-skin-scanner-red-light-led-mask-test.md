## The Science That Matters: Nanometers Over Names

Skin cells (specifically fibroblasts) don't care about sleek matte finishes, wireless apps, or luxury packaging. They only respond to two specific variables: **wavelength** and **irradiance**.

*   **Red Light (630–660 nm):** Reaches the surface layer of skin to boost cellular energy (ATP), increase turnover, and smooth fine lines.
*   **Near-Infrared Light (830–850 nm):** Penetrates deeper into the dermis and tissue to stimulate collagen synthesis, reduce inflammation, and accelerate recovery.

When light hits the skin at these exact nanometers with enough power output (**irradiance**, measured in mW/cm²), collagen production increases regardless of what brand logo is printed on the strap. 

Our clinical scans revealed that the $49 budget option delivered tight, high-intensity red (660nm) and near-infrared (850nm) LEDs directly against targeted skin areas. In contrast, several $400+ flexible silicone masks diffused light across too wide an area, yielding lower optical power density and slower dermal density gains over 30 days.

## Key Features to Look For (And What to Avoid)

Before buying any LED device, look past the marketing photos and check the technical specifications.

### What to Look For
1.  **Dual-Wavelength Chips:** Ensure the specs list both **660nm (Red)** and **850nm (Near-Infrared)**. Near-infrared is invisible to the human eye, but it does the heavy lifting for deep collagen growth.
2.  **Irradiance Metrics:** Aim for a target irradiance of at least **30 to 50 mW/cm²** at treatment distance. Higher irradiance means shorter treatment times.
3.  **Medical-Grade Eye Protection:** Look for built-in silicone eye shields or included blackout goggles to protect against glare.

### Common Mistakes
*   **Buying for "Color Variety":** Avoid cheap masks offering seven rainbow colors unless you have a specific, proven use case. Red and Near-Infrared are the only wavelengths backed by robust clinical data for anti-aging and collagen support.
*   **Prioritizing Comfort Over Output:** Soft silicone masks look cool and feel comfortable, but the LEDs are often spaced farther apart. Rigid panels or structured masks usually pack tighter LED clusters, offering higher power output per inch.
*   **Expecting Instant Magic:** Photobiomodulation works cumulatively. Skimmer results require 3–5 sessions per week for at least four to eight weeks.
