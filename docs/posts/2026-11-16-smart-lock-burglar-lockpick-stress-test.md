## Key Features That Actually Protect Your Door

When shopping for smart locks, don't get distracted by flashy features you'll never use. Look for these essential specs:

* **3D Capacitive Biometrics:** Cheap optical scanners read your thumbprint like a 2D photo, making them easy to bypass. Modern 3D capacitive sensors analyze the subtle electrical current in live skin—much like the biometric authentication on a premium smartphone. 
* **Solid Mechanical Foundations:** A smart lock is only as safe as its physical latch. Always look for an ANSI/BHMA Grade 1 or Grade 2 rating to ensure the internal deadbolt can withstand direct kick attacks.
* **Local Offline Encryption:** Avoid locks that rely entirely on cloud servers to register a simple unlock. AES-128 or 256-bit local Bluetooth/Z-Wave encryption keeps hackers from intercepting signals over your home Wi-Fi.
* **Anti-Peep Keypads & Auto-Locking:** Random PIN generator options prevent intruders from reading smudge marks on your touch panel—keeping your keypad as clean and fresh as a daily skincare routine.

## Common Smart Lock Mistakes to Avoid

Even the most secure lock will fail if installed incorrectly. Avoid these common mistakes:

* **Keeping the Stock Strike Plate:** Installing a brand-new smart lock into a flimsy builder-grade strike plate with 3/4-inch screws is like applying expensive facial serum without washing your face first. Swap them for 3-inch steel screws that anchor deep into the door frame stud.
* **Relying Solely on Wi-Fi:** Built-in Wi-Fi drains batteries fast and leaves you vulnerable during internet outages. Look for locks with Bluetooth, Zigbee, or local keypads.
* **Ignoring Emergency Power Options:** Always choose a lock equipped with exterior 9V battery contacts on the bottom of the keypad so you're never locked out by a dead battery.

## Which Smart Lock Tier Suits Your Home?

* **Budget Tier ($100–$150):** Best for renters, busy families, and single-family entryways. The right budget biometric lock delivers fast fingerprint unlocking, strong physical deadbolts, and straightforward access control without hidden monthly fees.
* **Mid-Range Tier ($180–$280):** Ideal for smart home enthusiasts. This range offers seamless integration with ecosystems like Apple Home, Zigbee, or Z-Wave, enabling custom home automation routines.
* **Premium Tier ($350+):** Useful for luxury rental hosts or multi-tenant properties needing built-in video cameras or cellular backup—though you are often paying a heavy premium for style over added physical protection.
