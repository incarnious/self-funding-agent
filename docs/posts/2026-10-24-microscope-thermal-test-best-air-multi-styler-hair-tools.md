## What the Thermal Imager Taught Us: Key Features to Look For

When shopping for an air blowout tool, most buyers look at the fancy attachments first. However, our thermal and air velocity tests revealed three core technical factors that truly dictate your results:

*   **Strict Temperature Capping:** Cheaper styling tools often claim to use "gentle air," but our thermal imager caught them spiking past 450°F within minutes. ## 3 Common Blowout Mistakes That Damage Hair

1.  **Styling Dripping-Wet Hair:** Air stylers are designed for damp hair (roughly 80% dry). Starting with soaking-wet strands takes too long to dry and exposes your hair to prolonged heat exposure.
2.  **Skipping Thermal Protectant:** Air-styling uses less heat than a 450°F flat iron, but protective serums or lightweight thermal sprays are still essential to coat the cuticle.
3.  **Forgetting the Cool Shot:** Heat softens the hair’s internal hydrogen bonds to shape it; cold air locks those bonds back in place. Always hold the cool-shot button for 5 to 10 seconds before releasing each curl section.
