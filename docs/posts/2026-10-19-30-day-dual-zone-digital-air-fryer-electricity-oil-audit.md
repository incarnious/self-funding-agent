## Decoding the Market: Budget vs. Mid-Range vs. Premium

If you are looking to make the switch, understanding how air fryers are tiered keeps you from overpaying for flashy gimmicks:

*   **Budget Tier ($50–$90):** Typically compact, single-basket models with basic digital touchpads or manual dials. They crisp food effectively but lack space and dual-cooking logic. Best suited for solo dorm living or basic side dishes.
*   **Mid-Range Tier ($120–$180):** The true sweet spot. Units in this range offer dual independent cooking zones, high-precision digital thermostats, and "sync-finish" controls. You can cook lean protein in one basket and vegetables in the other, timing them to complete simultaneously. 
*   **Premium Tier ($200+):** Includes features like integrated smart probes, steam-injection crisping, and mobile app monitoring. Useful for tech enthusiasts, but unnecessary for standard daily meal preparation.

## Features That Matter (And Mistakes to Avoid)

When shopping, prioritize **independent zone temperature control** and **ceramic-coated, non-stick removable plates**. Precise digital heat calibration matters—much like controlling water temperature during a sensitive facial wash to protect your skin barrier, exact digital heat stops lean meats from drying out while ensuring a crisp exterior.

**Common Mistakes to Watch For:**
1.  **Overcrowding the Baskets:** Stacking ingredients blocks airflow. Food steams instead of crisping—similar to layering too many thick face creams at once, which clogs pores instead of hydrating.
2.  **Aerosol Cooking Sprays:** Chemical propellants in standard cooking sprays react with non-stick coatings, causing them to degrade prematurely. Use a refillable oil mister instead.
3.  **Forgetting the Shake:** Always pause and shake your baskets halfway through the cycle to guarantee even heat distribution.
