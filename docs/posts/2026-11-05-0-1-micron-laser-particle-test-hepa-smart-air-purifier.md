## What to Look For (and Common Mistakes to Avoid)

When shopping for an air purifier for your bedroom or grooming space, don't pay for empty space or outdated tech. Focus on these critical features:

*   **True HEPA (H13 or H14 Rated):** Always check the label. Avoid vague terms like "HEPA-type" or "HEPA-like," which fail to catch ultra-fine PM0.1 particles. True H13 HEPA captures at least 99.97% of particles down to 0.3 microns and a massive percentage of 0.1-micron pollutants.
*   **Active Laser Auto-Sensing:** Cheap units use basic dust or odor sensors that miss fine particulate spikes. Real-time laser sensors adjust fan speeds the second you spray hairspray, cook dinner, or open a window during high-pollution days.
*   **High-Density Carbon Pellets:** Essential for trapping volatile organic compounds (VOCs), cologne mist, and lingering odors.

**The Most Common Mistake:** Buying an oversized, non-smart tower for a standard bedroom. Large medical towers are designed for massive open clinical spaces. In a residential bedroom, fast air-turnover rates paired with responsive smart algorithms matter far more than brute filter weight.
