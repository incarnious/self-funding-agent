## The $39 Budget Lock: Fast Convenience, Faster Bypass

At first glance, the $39 budget lock looks surprisingly sharp. It features a matte black finish that blends seamlessly into modern entryway decor. However, aesthetics don't stop an intruder.

Here is how the entry-level lock fared under attack:

* **Thermal Imager Test (Breached in 15 seconds):** The budget lock used soft silicone numbers. After typing a four-digit PIN, the user's body heat remained clearly visible on the keys through our thermal camera. The locksmith guessed the exact code on his first try simply by following the heat signatures from warmest to coolest button.
* **Bump Key Test (Breached in 4 seconds):** To maintain low production costs, budget smart locks often use cheap, standard physical key cylinders as a manual backup. Our locksmith inserted a standard bump key, tapped it lightly, and turned the cylinder almost instantly.
* **Crowbar Test (Breached in 8 seconds):** The exterior casing was made of thin zinc alloy with plastic mounting plates inside. A single strike with a pry bar sheared the mounting posts, exposing the interior turn-mechanism.

**The Verdict:** Budget smart locks are essentially glorified privacy latches. They offer great convenience for internal doors, but leave front entrances vulnerable.

## Key Security Features to Look For (And Mistakes to Avoid)

Whether you spend $50 or $500, a smart lock is only as secure as its underlying hardware.

### What to Look For:
1. **ANSI/BHMA Grade 1 or 2 Rating:** Look for certified commercial-grade durability. Avoid locks that do not publish their ANSI grade.
2. **Capacitive or Anti-Peep Keypads:** Choose touchscreens that randomize digits or dissipate thermal signatures quickly.
3. **Solid Core Deadbolts:** Ensure the internal throw bolt is solid steel, not hollow alloy.

### Common Mistakes:
* **Ignoring the Door Frame:** Installing a $450 lock with 3/4-inch screws into a soft pine door jamb is useless. Always upgrade your strike plate screws to 3-inch hardened steel screws that bite directly into the wall studs.
* **Leaving Default Master Codes:** Failing to change the factory admin PIN instantly opens your system to automated exploits.
