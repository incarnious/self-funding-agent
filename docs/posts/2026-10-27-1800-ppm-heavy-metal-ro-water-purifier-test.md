If you spend money on gentle cleansers, hydrating serums, or premium shaving balms, your tap water might be quietly working against you. ## Key Features to Look For (And Mistakes to Avoid)

When shopping for an RO and alkaline water purifier, skip the high-priced commercial marketing and prioritize these essentials:

1. **0.0001-Micron RO Membrane:** This is the gold standard for trapping microplastics, lead, arsenic, and PFAS.
2. **Post-RO Alkaline Remineralization:** Pure RO water can be slightly acidic (pH 5.5–6.5), which tastes flat and can feel drying on sensitive skin. A remineralization stage adds trace calcium, magnesium, and potassium, raising the pH to a skin-friendly, hydrating 7.5–8.5.
3. **Low-Waste Ratio:** Look for modern systems offering a 1:1 or 2:1 pure-to-waste water ratio to keep your utility bills low.

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
* **Buying for Speed Over Purity:** Ultra-fast flow rates sound convenient, but slower filtration allows for deeper purification.
* **Forgetting Filter Replacement Costs:** A budget system is only a deal if annual replacement filters stay under $50–$80 per year.
