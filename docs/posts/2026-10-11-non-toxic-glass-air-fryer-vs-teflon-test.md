## Why Ditch the Teflon Basket?

Traditional air fryers hide your food behind dark metal walls and require constant basket-shaking. Glass health cookers flip the script:

* **Zero Chemical Off-Gassing:** Pure tempered glass contains no PFAS, PFOA, heavy metals, or plasticizers that can degrade under high heat.
* **360-Degree Visibility:** Just like adding a smart sensor to your home setup, clear walls let you monitor browning in real time without pulling the basket and dumping precious heat.
* **Effortless Cleaning:** Baked-on grease wipes off smooth glass far easier than from degrading non-stick coatings, keeping your post-dinner cleanup as low-maintenance as a simple two-step grooming routine.

## Key Features to Look For (And Mistakes to Avoid)

**What to Look For:**
* **Element Type:** Look for combined halogen and convection systems. Halogen heats instantly—no preheating required.
* **Hinged vs. Detachable Lid:** Detachable lids save counter height under low cabinets, but hinged lids keep hot heating elements safely attached to the base.
* **Capacity:** A 3.5 to 5-quart glass bowl handles daily sides, but look for models with an included stainless-steel extender ring if you roast whole 5 lb chickens regularly.

**Common Mistakes:**
* **Thermal Shock:** Never place a hot glass bowl directly onto a cold marble countertop or run cold water over it immediately after cooking. Always use the included silicone pad or trivet.
* **Crowding the Bowl:** Piling chicken pieces on top of each other blocks air circulation. Leave a slight gap between cuts so the hot air can wrap around the food for an all-over crunch.
