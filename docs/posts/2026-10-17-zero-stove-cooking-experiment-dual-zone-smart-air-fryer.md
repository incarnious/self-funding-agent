## Key Features That Made the Experiment Work

If you want to swap out your cooktop entirely, a simple single-basket unit won't suffice. You need specific features that can mimic a full meal prep workflow on your counter.

*   **Independent Dual Zones:** The ability to set two distinct temperatures and timers simultaneously is non-negotiable. You can roast salmon at 390°F in Basket A while gently crisping asparagus at 350°F in Basket B.
*   **Smart Syncing Technology:** This setting automatically holds back the faster-cooking zone so both baskets complete their cycles at the exact same moment. No more cold side dishes waiting on your main protein.
*   **Mobile App Connectivity:** Wi-Fi notifications mean you don't have to stand over a hot stove. You can step into the bathroom to complete your evening face wash and skin care regimen while your dinner crisps, receiving a ping when it's time to shake the basket.

When shopping, consider how different tiers approach these features:
*   **Budget Tiers:** Offer basic dual-basket setups with manual dials, but typically lack smart synchronization features and app connectivity.
*   **Mid-Range Tiers:** The sweet spot for most home cooks. These include digital touchscreens, smart sync modes, app alerts, and durable non-stick baskets that clean in seconds.
*   **Premium Tiers:** Add integrated smart temperature probes, steam-assisted crisping functions, and automated cook-program presets.

## Rookie Mistakes That Ruin Air Fryer Meals

Transitioning away from a traditional stove requires shifting your cooking habits. Avoid these three common mistakes:

1.  **Overcrowding the Baskets:** Air fryers rely on rapid convection heat. Packing chicken cutlets tightly or overloading frozen fries traps steam, resulting in soggy textures rather than crisp edges. Give your food breathing room.
2.  **Using Aerosol Cooking Sprays:** Propellants in canned spray oils can degrade the non-stick coating over time. Instead, use a refillable mister filled with high-smoke-point oils like avocado or light olive oil.
3.  **Forgetting to Flip or Shake:** Even with dual heating elements, flipping your proteins or shaking your vegetable baskets halfway through ensures an even, golden finish across the board.
