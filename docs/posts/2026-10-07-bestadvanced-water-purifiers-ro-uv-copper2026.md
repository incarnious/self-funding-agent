## Why RO + UV + Copper is the Modern Standard

Single-stage or dual-stage filters often fall short against heavy metals, fluctuating TDS (Total Dissolved Solids), and seasonal bacterial spikes. 

*   **RO (Reverse Osmosis):** Removes dissolved salts, microplastics, arsenic, and lead. Essential if your water comes from borewells or tankers.
*   **UV (Ultraviolet):** Neutralizes 99.9% of viruses and bacteria, ensuring water stored in the tank remains sterile.
*   **Copper Infusion:** Traditional copper vessels take hours to leach minerals into water. Modern purifiers instantly infuse active copper ions, helping combat oxidative stress, aiding digestion, and supporting clearer skin and hair health directly from the tap.

When integrated into your kitchen, these triple-action systems function as both a health safeguard and a slick smart home accessory.

## Key Features to Look For (And Common Mistakes to Avoid)

### What to Look For:
1.  **Active Copper Dosing:** Look for systems with active or electro-chemical copper infusion rather than basic copper-coated filter pads, which lose effectiveness after a few months.
2.  **TDS Control / Mineralizers:** Pure RO water can taste flat and lack essential minerals. A reliable TDS controller restores magnesium and calcium to ideal levels.
3.  **Smart Display & App Alerts:** High-end models connect to your home Wi-Fi or feature LED indicators showing real-time water quality, filter life, and maintenance reminders.

### Mistakes to Avoid:
*   **Over-purifying Low-TDS Water:** If your municipal water TDS is already below 200 ppm, a pure RO system without a TDS controller can strip away beneficial minerals.
*   **Ignoring Water Recovery Rates:** Standard RO purifiers waste up to two cups of water for every cup purified. Look for "High Recovery" or "Zero Water Wastage" technologies that recycle reject water back into your main tank.
*   **Forgetting Annual Service Costs:** Always factor in filter replacement fees. A cheap purifier with expensive annual filters quickly becomes a money pit.
