## What to Look for in a High-Impact Carry-On

When shopping for a cabin trolley that can survive real-world baggage handlers and cobblestone streets, prioritize these key components:

1.  **100% Polycarbonate Shells:** Avoid cheap ABS plastic or "polycarbonate blends," which crack easily under cold temperatures or heavy drops. Look for raw, unpainted virgin polycarbonate.
2.  **Steel-Axle Japanese Spinner Wheels:** Cobblestone streets melt cheap plastic wheel housings. Look for dual-spinner wheels with reinforced steel axles that dampen vibration.
3.  **Self-Healing Zippers vs. Latch Frames:** High-tensile woven zippers give the bag margin to flex. Rigid metal latches look high-end, but once bent a fraction of a millimeter, they fail completely.
4.  **Water-Resistant Spill Compartments:** Essential for housing men's grooming liquids, face oils, and cologne safely away from your tailored clothes.

## 3 Common Mistakes Buyers Make

1.  **Confusing Hardness with Toughness:** Buying cheap ABS plastic because it feels "stiff." Stiff plastic shatters; flexible polycarbonate bends and survives.
2.  **Ignoring Wheel Track Width:** Narrowly spaced wheels cause carry-ons to tip over on uneven cobblestones, putting unnecessary strain on your wrist and handle.
3.  **Forgetting Internal Padding:** A tough exterior means nothing if your internal organization allows your heavy electric razor to crush your expensive face moisturizer. Look for bags with built-in padded dividers.
