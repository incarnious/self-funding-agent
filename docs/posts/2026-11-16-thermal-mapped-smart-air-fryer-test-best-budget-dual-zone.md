## What Thermal Imaging Revealed About Heat Distribution

When viewing an air fryer through a thermal camera, you instantly see why some appliances produce soggy centers while others yield perfect crunch.

## Key Features to Look For (and Mistakes to Avoid)

When shopping for smart kitchen appliances, don't get blinded by app connectivity or bloated touchscreens. Look for structural design efficiency:

*   **Basket Geometry (Wide vs. Deep):** A wider surface area beats a deep basket every time. Depth forces you to stack food, creating moisture traps. Wide baskets allow heat to wrap around each piece 360 degrees, similar to how an exfoliating liquid toner covers every pore evenly.
*   **Dual-Zone Independent Control:** Having two smaller, isolated chambers allows you to cook protein at high heat on one side while gently warming sides on the other without heat bleed.
*   **Refinement of Airflow Grates:** Look for crisping plates with high-percentage open ventilation rather than solid trays with small side slots.

### Common Air Fryer Mistakes
1.  **Overcrowding the Basket:** Stacking frozen fries creates a steam chamber. If the heat camera shows red on top and blue underneath, your food is steaming, not frying.
2.  **Skipping the 2-Minute Preheat:** Just as you wouldn’t apply serum to unwashed skin or shave without warming your face, putting frozen food into a cold chamber slows down Maillard browning and leads to soggy breading.
3.  **Relying Blindly on "Smart" Presets:** Preset buttons often over-predict cooking time and under-predict temperature. Manual control based on visual cues always wins.
