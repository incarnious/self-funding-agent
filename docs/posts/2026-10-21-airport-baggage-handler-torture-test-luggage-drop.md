## The Roof Drop Results: Polycarbonate Tiers Tested

### 1. The Budget Tier (ABS / Polycarbonate Blend)
* **The Drop:** The shell flexed violently on impact. While it didn't shatter into pieces, the corner split along the seam, and the single-coil zipper burst wide open, scattering packed items across the driveway. 
* **The Verdict:** Budget blends rely heavily on ABS plastic, which is rigid but brittle. If you pack glass skincare bottles or delicate electronics, a hard drop will transfer that force straight inside.

### 2. The Mid-Range Tier (100% Virgin Polycarbonate with Double-Coil Zippers)
* **The Drop:** It hit the asphalt with a loud thud, bounced twice, and rolled. The matte shell suffered a few minor scuffs—easily wiped away much like cleaning a silicone beauty blender—but zero cracks. The puncture-resistant double zipper held completely secure.
* **The Verdict:** This is the absolute sweet spot for value. The pure polycarbonate shell flexed under impact to absorb the shock, leaving the delicate face creams and glass cologne bottles inside completely intact.

### 3. The Premium Tier (Reinforced Polycarbonate with Aluminum Frame)
* **The Drop:** Featuring a heavy-duty zipperless aluminum latch system, this set landed directly on its reinforced metal corner cap. Dent on the metal trim? Yes. Structural failure? None.
* **The Verdict:** Total tank-like protection. However, considering it costs significantly more than the mid-range set, it's only necessary for extreme conditions.

## Key Features: What to Look For Before You Buy

* **100% Polycarbonate vs. ABS Blends:** Always check the fine print. Pure polycarbonate behaves like high-performance skincare packaging: resilient, flexible under pressure, and highly impact-absorbent. ABS is cheaper but crack-prone in freezing cargo holds.
* **Burst-Proof Zippers:** Look for double-coil (puncture-resistant) zippers. A pen test can easily pop a standard zipper open, but double coils lock tightly under pressure.
* **Waterproof Internal Dividers:** Great luggage protects your clothes from outer rain, but it should also protect your wardrobe from internal leaks. Dedicated PVC-lined wet pockets are crucial for housing face oils, liquid foundations, and shaving creams.

---

## Common Luggage Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Choosing Glossy Over Textured Finishes:** High-gloss shells look stunning on the display floor, but like an un-powdered face on a humid day, they show every swipe, smudge, and scratch after one flight. Opt for micro-diamond or matte textures to hide tarmac wear.
2. **Overpacking Beyond Zipper Tolerances:** Forcing a luggage zipper to stretch beyond its design stresses the teeth. If you must overpack, choose a mid-range set with built-in expansion joints.
