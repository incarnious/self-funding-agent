## Key Features: What Real Spatial Audio Actually Requires

Before dropping your hard-earned cash on a system based on aesthetics alone, look past the brushed-aluminum exterior and verify these hardware essentials:

*   **Physical Up-Firing Drivers:** Just like daily SPF is a non-negotiable step in a solid skincare routine, physical drivers angled at your ceiling (typically 20 degrees) are essential for real height channels. 
*   **Dedicated Rear Satellites:** Virtual surround sound is the audio equivalent of a 2-in-1 shampoo and conditioner—it promises convenience, but masterfully performs neither. Separate rear channels create a genuine 360-degree sound bubble.
*   **HDMI eARC Support:** Compressed audio ruins detail. Ensure your system supports HDMI eARC to pass uncompromised Dolby TrueHD signals from your TV.

## Avoid These 3 Common Setup Mistakes

1.  **Tucking the Bar Inside a Console:** Placing your soundbar inside a wooden cabinet blocks side-firing and up-firing drivers. It’s like applying expensive moisturizer over a dry clay mask—nothing gets through.
2.  **Ignoring Ceiling Height and Texture:** Up-firing Atmos relies on smooth, flat ceilings (8 to 12 feet high). Vaulted or heavily textured popcorn ceilings scatter soundwaves wildly.
3.  **Skipping Manual Level Calibration:** Never rely purely on out-of-the-box settings. Boost your height and rear channels by +2 or +3 dB to compensate for room distance.
