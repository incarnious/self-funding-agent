## What to Look for in a True Clinical LED Mask

Not all light therapy devices are created equal. To see measurable changes under a Visia scanner, a mask must meet three strict technical standards:

*   **Targeted Wavelengths:** Look for precise wavelengths. Red light at **633 nanometers (nm)** triggers collagen production, while Near-Infrared (NIR) light at **830 nm** penetrates deeper subcutaneous layers to calm inflammation and boost cellular energy.
*   **Irradiance (Power Output):** A device needs an irradiance of 30 to 50 mW/cm² to deliver therapeutic light energy without overheating the skin.
*   **Contoured Contact:** Light energy dissipates rapidly with distance. Devices that sit directly against the skin deliver photons far more efficiently than rigid frames mounted away from the face.

## Common LED Light Therapy Mistakes to Avoid

1.  **Applying Barrier Creams Before Treatment:** Heavy occlusives, sunscreens, and opaque oils reflect light energy away from the skin. Always use your LED mask on clean, dry skin or over a light, non-reflective hydration serum.
2.  **Ignoring Near-Infrared (NIR) Light:** Red light handles surface texture, but NIR (invisible to the human eye) is essential for deep collagen remodeling. Ensure your mask includes dedicated 830nm diodes.
3.  **Inconsistent Usage:** LED therapy relies on cumulative cellular energy build-up. Skipping days reduces efficacy—consistency matters far more than session duration.
