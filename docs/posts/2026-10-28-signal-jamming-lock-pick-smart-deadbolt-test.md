## What Happens in a Breach Test (And Why Pretty Interfaces Lie)

When auditing a smart lock, you aren't just testing code—you are testing metallurgy, pin physics, and signal resilience. Intruders generally choose the path of least resistance: opportunists use RF jammers to block "auto-lock" commands or app alerts, while physical trespassers target cheap linear pins with bump keys.

The most common mistake homeowners make is buying a lock based purely on aesthetic minimalism. If a lock's internal motor stalls when a door settles, or if its manual keyway uses standard bottom pins without anti-pick spooling, that sleek glass interface is just cosmetic coverage over a structural flaw.

## Smart Lock Shopping: Skincare Rules Apply

When refining your home entryway, treat it like an essential daily routine:

* **Prioritize "Active Ingredients" (Local Processing):** Ensure the lock handles biometric and PIN data locally on the device rather than relying 100% on cloud servers.
* **Check the Mechanical Rating:** Always look for ANSI/BHMA Grade 1 or Grade 2 ratings. The smart features provide daily convenience, but solid metal stops the crowbar.
* **Maintain Your Barrier:** Just like skipping daily SPF damages your skin over time, ignoring low-battery warnings or installing a lock on a misaligned door frame strains the motor and compromises your safety.
